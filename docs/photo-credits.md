## Generated media

These were generated with Google Flow (Veo for video) from the prompts in `docs/redesign-plan.md`. They show no real
job, person or place. They are illustrative in the same way as the stock photos: fine while `demoMode` is on and the
"Sample" labels show, and to be replaced with the client's own footage and job photos on a live site.

| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| `public/videos/ft-hero-site*.mp4`, poster `hero-poster` | Home hero background; the poster is also the share image |
| `public/videos/ft-craft-joinery*.mp4`, poster `cta-poster` | Closing "Plan your project" band |
| `public/videos/ft-showcase-kitchen*.mp4`, poster `showcase-poster` | Kitchen interlude on the home page |
| `project-penrith-kitchen-renovation` and `-before` | The sample before/after slider and the Penrith sample project card. One generated room, edited into its "before" state, so the two frames line up |

Notes:
- Photos were captured at screen resolution (1366 px wide, 1000 px for portraits), cropped to each slot's ratio, and saved as JPEG. The build serves them as AVIF/WebP.
- The area photos are general Australian suburban streets, except Parramatta (the real Town Hall). Replace them with real local shots for a live client.
